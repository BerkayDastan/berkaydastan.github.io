Ember Rush:
Fire Jump

Keep your flame alive. Climb higher, dodge obstacles, escape rising lava and challenge your high score.

Download on the App Store

How to play

Tap Play to begin. Tap the left half of the screen to fly left and upward, or the right half to fly right and upward. Avoid obstacles and rising lava.

Collect embers to unlock cosmetic flame characters. Enjoy endless gameplay with increasing difficulty, quick restarts, and Turkish and English language options.

Core gameplay works offline. Advertisements require an internet connection. Rewarded ads are optional.

Alevini canlı tut!

Ember Rush, yükselen lavdan kaçıp engelleri aşarak kendi rekorunu geliştirdiğin sonsuz bir arcade oyunudur.
